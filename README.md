# EE 211 Circuits — study site

An unofficial, interactive study companion to Eric Hernandez's EE 211 lecture videos (CSULB): Δ–Y conversions,
Thévenin & Norton equivalents, source transformations, Millman's theorem, capacitors/inductors and RC/RL circuits,
and oscilloscope measurements.

Open the site: https://gozorp.github.io/ee211-study/

* The lecture videos are embedded from YouTube and belong to their owner.
* Transcripts are YouTube's automatic captions with corrected technical terms; they are not verbatim transcripts.
* Your notes and progress are stored only in your own browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent to this repository.
